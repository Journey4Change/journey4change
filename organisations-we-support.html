Organisations we walk alongside

Journey4Change doesn't just raise awareness — every province we cross, we look for the people already doing the work on the ground. These are two of the organisations we proudly support and amplify along the way.

Our Partner Organisations

Grassroots Work, Real Impact

From safe shelters for survivors of abuse to youth development in rural communities, these NPOs represent the everyday courage that mental health awareness is built on.

Child Protection & Victim Empowerment

Valerie House

Mooinooi, Madibeng, North West Province

A home for the forgotten. Valerie House is a Non-Profit Organisation and Public Benefit Organisation supporting women and children affected by abuse and violence, offering temporary safe shelter and victim empowerment services for survivors of crime and gender-based violence.

  • Founder & CEO: Valerie Mahlatsi
  • Focus: Child protection, temporary safe shelters, GBV victim support
  • Status: NPO & PBO, South Africa

Youth Development & Community Empowerment

Ulusha Lolwazi NPC

Verena, Mpumalanga Province

Igniting minds, transforming futures. Ulusha Lolwazi Youth Development Centre is a self-funded, SARS-compliant non-profit company serving the Verena community, focused on local youth development, community empowerment, and support initiatives including sanitary pad drives for girl learners.

  • Registration: 2026/2797/13
  • Focus: Youth development, community empowerment, dignity drives for girl learners
  • Status: Self-funded, SARS-compliant NPC
