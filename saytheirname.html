Gender-Based Violence & Femicide

SAY THEIR NAMES

These women were killed in South Africa. Some of their killers are behind bars. Others have never been found. Their deaths were not statistics they were daughters, students, mothers and friends, and their families are still waiting for the justice, or simply the peace, that comes from knowing the truth.

Case Solved Cape Town · August 2019

Uyinene Mrwetyana

19 years old · UCT film & media student

Uyinene went to collect a parcel at her local post office in Claremont and never came home. She was raped and killed by a post office employee, Luyanda Botha, who had lured her there after hours. Her death sparked the #AmINext movement and nationwide protests against gender-based violence.

The Case

Luyanda Botha pleaded guilty and was sentenced to three life terms in November 2019. He is not eligible for parole for 25 years.

Case Solved Johannesburg · June 2020

Tshegofatso Pule

28 years old · Beauty products representative

Eight months pregnant, Tshegofatso was killed in Roodepoort in June 2020, her body found hanging from a tree. It emerged that her boyfriend, Ntuthuko Shoba, had hired a hitman to kill her rather than let his wife learn of the pregnancy.

The Case

The hitman, Muzikayise Malephane, was sentenced to 20 years after pleading guilty. Ntuthuko Shoba was convicted of masterminding the murder and sentenced to life imprisonment in July 2022.

Case Solved Johannesburg · April 2017

Karabo Mokoena

22 years old

Karabo's remains were found in an open field in Lyndhurst, Johannesburg in April 2017. Her ex-boyfriend, Sandile Mantsoe, had killed her and attempted to destroy the evidence. Her murder helped ignite the wave of #MenAreTrash protests that year.

The Case

Sandile Mantsoe was convicted of murder, assault and defeating the ends of justice, and sentenced to an effective 32 years in prison in May 2018.

Case Solved Bredasdorp · February 2013

Anene Booysen

17 years old

Anene was attacked near a construction site on her way home from a night out in Bredasdorp. She survived long enough to name her attacker before she died in hospital, telling police that several men had been involved. Her death became one of the defining cases in South Africa's fight against gender-based violence.

The Case

Johannes Kana was convicted of rape and murder and sentenced to two life terms in 2013. Although Anene named more than one attacker before she died, no one else was ever charged.

Case Solved East London · August 2021

Nosicelo Mtebeni

23 years old · Final-year LLB student, Fort Hare

Nosicelo was in her final year of a law degree when she was killed by her boyfriend, Alutha Pasile, at their shared home in East London after a jealous confrontation. Her death, weeks into South Africa's annual 16 Days of Activism campaign, drew national outrage.

The Case

Alutha Pasile pleaded guilty to murder and was sentenced to 25 years in prison in December 2021. Her family said publicly they felt the sentence was too lenient for what he did.

Case Solved Pretoria · February 2013

Reeva Steenkamp

29 years old · Model and law graduate

Reeva was shot by her boyfriend, Paralympian Oscar Pistorius, through a locked bathroom door at his Pretoria home on Valentine's Day 2013. He claimed he mistook her for an intruder — a defence prosecutors and the courts ultimately rejected.

The Case

Pistorius was convicted of murder on appeal and sentenced to 13 years and 5 months. He was released on parole in January 2024 after serving roughly half his sentence.

Unsolved Soweto · May 2017

Popi Qwabe

24 years old · Aspiring actress

Popi and her friend Bongeka Phungula climbed into a minibus taxi one Friday night in Johannesburg and were never heard from again. Popi's body was found the next day; she had been shot. The two friends had met studying drama together and dreamed of making it as actors.

The Case

Two taxi drivers found with the women's belongings were arrested but later released for lack of evidence. In 2023, more than 6 years on, the NPA said it still did not have enough evidence to prosecute anyone. No one has ever been convicted.

Unsolved Soweto · May 2017

Bongeka Phungula

28 years old · Aspiring actress

Bongeka was killed alongside her close friend Popi Qwabe on the same night in May 2017, her body found the following day in a neighbouring part of Soweto. Their families have spent years pushing for a proper investigation into what happened to them.

The Case

As with Popi's case, the two suspects initially arrested were released, and the NPA has said there is not enough evidence to prosecute anyone. The case was referred for an inquest in 2023. No one has ever been convicted.

Why Solved Or Not Matters

A Conviction Doesn't Undo the Loss But an Unsolved Case Compounds It

For the families above whose cases were solved, a conviction never brings their daughter, sister or friend back. But it does something else: it tells them, and the country, that this loss was seen, investigated, and taken seriously by the justice system.

For families like Popi's and Bongeka's, there is no such closure only years of chasing a docket that keeps stalling. Every unsolved case is also a warning left unaddressed, a perpetrator who was never named, and a signal to others that this kind of violence can go unpunished.

Evidence Gets Lost
Delayed forensic testing, missing dockets, and untraced cellphone records have derailed multiple high-profile GBVF cases in South Africa.
Under-Resourced Units
Family Violence, Child Protection and Sexual Offences (FCS) units are frequently stretched thin, slowing investigations before charges are even possible.
Public Pressure Can Help
Several of the solved cases above only reached trial after sustained media coverage and public protest kept the investigation from stalling.
Families Keep Pushing
In every unsolved case, it is almost always the victim's family and friends not the system who keep the pressure on for answers.
