Verified figures on mental health, gender-based violence, substance abuse and homelessness in South Africa, drawn from Stats SA, WHO and peer-reviewed sources.

Understanding the Challenges

Mental Health & Social Challenges

These figures come from Stats SA's Governance, Public Safety and Justice Survey, the South African Demographic and Health Survey, WHO estimates, and published South African research. Each is a different measure, on a different population and year — they are not directly comparable, so they should not be read as a single ranking.

Mental Health

Depression remains one of the most common mental health conditions in South Africa, alongside anxiety, PTSD, bipolar disorder and schizophrenia.

Gender-Based Violence

Around one in five South African women have experienced physical violence by a partner, and alcohol is a frequent factor in reported abuse incidents.

Community Wellbeing

Homelessness and substance abuse continue to affect communities nationwide, as shown in the highlight figures below the main chart.

VERIFIED SOURCES
Read this carefully: These figures are not one single prevalence measure. They combine different indicators, populations and years, so the bars should not be interpreted as a ranking. Each bar is labelled with what the percentage actually measures and its source year.

Sources: World Health Organization Global Health Estimates (based on the 2010 Global Burden of Disease study); Statistics South Africa, Governance, Public Safety and Justice Survey (GPSJS) 2024/25; Statistics South Africa, "Crimes against Women in South Africa" (2017), drawing on the 2016 South Africa Demographic and Health Survey; Department of Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation, "Synthesis Report: Gender-Based Violence, Alcohol Abuse and Pandemics" (2020).

What each bar measures: depression prevalence is a WHO/Global Burden of Disease modelled estimate for South Africa's general population; the "experienced partner violence" figure is the share of South African women aged 18+ who report ever having experienced physical violence by a partner; the "partner intoxication" figure is the share of women who experienced partner abuse in the past 12 months who also reported the partner was intoxicated at the time; the two psychological violence figures come from Stats SA's most recent GPSJS release (2024/25) and cover individuals aged 16 and older.

23.5 per 100,000

South Africa's suicide mortality rate in 2019 (WHO / World Bank data) — more than double the global average of roughly 9 per 100,000.

55,719 people

Counted as homeless in South Africa's 2022 Census (Stats SA), with substance abuse cited as a contributing factor in several provinces.
