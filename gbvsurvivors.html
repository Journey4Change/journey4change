South Africans Who Chose to Speak

Survivor Port Elizabeth · Attacked 1994

Alison Botha

Author, "I Have Life" · International Speaker

In December 1994, Alison was abducted, raped and left for dead after a brutal attack that nearly killed her. She survived against every medical expectation and became one of the first South African rape survivors to publicly use her own name and face testifying against her attackers, both convicted and sentenced to life imprisonment.

"There was so much I still wanted to do, so much that I still wanted to live for."
Now a motivational speaker in over 30 countries.
Survivor Hammanskraal · SANDF Journalist

Captain Tebogo August

Author, "No Longer Wearing the Victim Badge"

Tebogo was sexually abused by a close family member at the age of six. Decades later, now a military journalist with the SANDF, she wrote a book about her journey through bullying, abuse and shame choosing to reconcile with her past instead of being defined by it.

"I had to acknowledge that all of who I am is indeed who I am... instead of asking 'Why me?', I forgave and reconciled."
Uses her story to help other survivors find their voice.
Survivor South Africa · Shared her story in 2025

Bukelwa Moerane

Escaped an attempted abduction and rape

Bukelwa escaped a would-be rapist by jumping from his moving car, leaving her covered in scrapes and bruises. Years later, reading about the murder of Karabo Mokoena brought her own ordeal flooding back and she decided her story, too, deserved to be told.

She knows her story and Karabo's are very different she survived, while Karabo never came home.
Turned a painful memory into a story that might help someone else.
Survivor South Africa · Spoke publicly in 2025

Shoki Mmola

Award-Winning Actress

Shoki shared reflections from her past marriage during a keynote address on gender-based violence, reminding audiences that abuse can affect anyone regardless of success, confidence or public image and that speaking out is the first step toward accountability.

"I never imagined that I would one day be here sharing this kind of story... silence isolates survivors and protects abusers."
Now calls on workplaces to build safer spaces for disclosure.
Survivor South Africa · Advocate

Bulelwa Adonis

Spokesperson, Women for Change South Africa

Bulelwa has spoken openly about her own experience of trauma alongside her work as spokesperson for Women for Change, a grassroots movement that has supported survivors with counselling, legal aid and community since 2016 turning her personal pain into a national platform.

"We are motivated by the fact that if we don't do it, who will do it..."
Helps other survivors feel believed and heard.
Survivor Etwatwa, Gauteng

Nelisiwe Maphlanga

Mother · GBV Ambassador, Be the Voice

A mother from Etwatwa on the East Rand, Nelisiwe carried the emotional and psychological weight of gender-based violence in silence for years. Through a community leadership programme she began speaking out, even before she felt fully healed, to support other women still finding their voice.

Even before she had fully healed, she felt a strong pull to speak out and support other survivors.
Now a role model in her community.
Your Voice Matters

Share Your Story

Whatever happened to you, and however long ago if you are ready to talk about it, we want to make space for that. You choose how much to share, whether to use your name, and how your story is used.

Email Us Directly

Write to us whenever you're ready. No word count, no pressure share as much or as little as feels right.

Stay Anonymous

Let us know if you'd like your name withheld or changed. Your safety and comfort always come first.

Talk It Through First

Not sure you're ready to write it down? Call SADAG or the GBV Command Centre to talk it through first.

Email Your Story GBV Support & Resources

Every story submitted is read by a real person on our team before anything is published, and nothing goes online without your permission. If sharing your story brings up difficult emotions, please also reach out to SADAG on 0800 567 567 you don't have to process this alone.
