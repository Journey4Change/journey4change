They're Still Here

They Survived. They're Still Here.

Every person on this page once believed there was no way through. They were wrong and they chose to say so publicly, so that someone else struggling today might believe it too.

Honouring 12 survivors and everyone still fighting, unseen, today.

Sbahle Mpisane

Survived multiple suicide attempts, 2019–2021

After a near-fatal car accident in 2018 left her with life-changing injuries, Sbahle survived several suicide attempts during her recovery. She has since spoken openly on Instagram about therapy, medication, and faith: "I still don't understand my survival, but maybe there's a purpose for it all."

Still training, still healing, still here.
Survivor South Africa Actress · 7de Laan

Nobuhle "Mimi" Mahlasela

Spoke publicly in 2021

The actress revealed she had survived two suicide attempts while privately battling depression. She now credits reaching out instead of staying silent with turning things around: "That simple act of calling someone was me fighting!"

Chose to keep fighting, one day at a time.
Survivor South Africa Gospel Singer · Campaigner

Hlengiwe Mhlaba

Survived an attempt before fame

Long before she found success, Hlengiwe survived a suicide attempt during a period of family conflict and financial desperation: "Luckily, I was saved in the nick of time." She now campaigns with Ungazibulali, using music to reach others in crisis.

Turned her survival into a mission for others.
Survivor International Actress · The Good Place

Jameela Jamil

Spoke publicly in 2019

Jamil has spoken openly about surviving a suicide attempt years earlier and treating the PTSD that followed with EMDR therapy. On World Suicide Prevention Day she wrote: "Surviving suicide has been the most extraordinary gift... please hang on."

Uses her platform to tell others to hang on.
Survivor International Actor · Prison Break

Wentworth Miller

Spoke publicly in 2013

Miller survived a suicide attempt at 15 while struggling in silence to accept his sexuality. Years later, after coming out publicly, he shared his story to a room full of strangers: "You only cry for help if you believe there's help to cry for."

Broke his silence to help someone else break theirs.
Survivor International Actress · TV Host

Drew Barrymore

Survived an attempt at 14

As a teenage former child star struggling with addiction, Barrymore survived a suicide attempt at 14. She wrote about that period in her bestselling memoir, "Little Girl Lost," and has spent decades since building the career and life she once nearly lost.

Went on to build a life she's proud of.
Survivor International TV Personality

Sharon Osbourne

Spoke publicly in 2019

Osbourne revealed on a morning show that she had survived three suicide attempts earlier in her life, describing one moment that shocked her into choosing to keep going: "I just couldn't bear it." She has managed her mental health with ongoing treatment ever since.

Chose her family, again and again.
Survivor International Singer · TV Personality

Tamar Braxton

Spoke publicly in 2020

After surviving a suicide attempt in the middle of a depressive episode, Braxton gave her first interview describing it as "my lowest point of life." She later posted that it was only by grace that she was still here to use her voice.

Found her voice again after her lowest point.
Survivor International Actress · Crazy Rich Asians

Constance Wu

Spoke publicly in 2022

Following intense online backlash in 2019, Wu survived a suicide attempt and was found in time by a friend who rushed her to hospital. Looking back, she said: "It was a scary moment that made me reassess a lot in my life."

A friend's quick action gave her a second chance.
Survivor International Singer

Madison Beer

Spoke publicly in 2021

After a long period of suicidal ideation as a teenager, Madison survived an attempt to take her own life. She has since said the experience clarified something important: "I made the choice to live." She now speaks openly to young fans about mental health.

Chose to live — and says so, out loud.
Survivor International Rapper · Italy

Fedez

Spoke publicly in 2024

Decades after surviving a suicide attempt at 18 during a troubled, drug-affected period of his life, the Italian rapper and cancer survivor spoke about it on national television: "I attempted suicide when I was 18." His openness sparked a national conversation on men's mental health.

Broke a two-decade silence to help other young men.
Survivor International Rapper

Lil Wayne

Spoke publicly in 2018

Lil Wayne survived a suicide attempt as a preteen, a period of his life he later wrote a song about. When asked about going public with it, following the deaths of Anthony Bourdain and Kate Spade, he said the track had been finished for years he simply hadn't been ready to share it.

Took years to speak, and it still mattered.
What Their Stories Teach Us

Survival Is Possible and It Rarely Happens Alone

None of the people above got through their darkest moment by themselves. A friend called an ambulance. A sister answered the phone. A doctor stayed five extra minutes. A stranger at a support group said the right thing at the right time. Every one of these stories has someone else in it.

That is the pattern worth noticing: reaching out or being reached is what turns an attempt into a survival story instead of an ending. In South Africa, where stigma keeps so many suffering in silence, hearing public figures say "I nearly didn't make it, and I'm glad I did" can be the thing that gives someone else permission to ask for help.

Someone Noticed
In nearly every story here, another person a friend, family member, or stranger recognised the danger and acted, often just in time.
They Eventually Spoke
Whether it took days or decades, every survivor here reached a point where they chose to talk about it to a friend, a therapist, or the world.
Ongoing Support Helped
Therapy, medication, rehab, and community all appear repeatedly in these stories survival is rarely a single moment, but a continued process.
A Reason Emerged
Many describe finding a reason to keep going only after surviving a child, a craft, a cause that they could not have seen from inside the crisis.
