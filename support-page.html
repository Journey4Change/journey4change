The Journey Continues

From Awareness to Action

Journey4Change is designed to be a bridge. When someone needs support beyond what this website provides, they can follow an external link to an appropriate service.

1

Learn

Understand the issue and recognise when support may be needed.

2

Reach Out

Use a trusted support organisation or professional service.

3

Connect

Find community, family and practical support around the person.

4

Keep Walking

Continue building healthier, safer and more supported communities.

Important: Journey4Change is not a replacement for emergency services, medical care, counselling, legal services or professional treatment. External links take you to the relevant organisation's own website.
